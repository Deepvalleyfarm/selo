# Selonachipa Rider and Agent Terms

_Last updated: 27 November 2026_

## 1. Scope and Independent Status

1.1. These Rider and Agent Terms apply to any person who carries out deliveries through Selonachipa (a **Rider**) or facilitates orders, payments, collections or sign-ups on the Platform (an **Agent**). Selonachipa is owned and operated by **Deep Valley Farms Limited** (the **Company**).

1.2. These terms add to the [Selonachipa Terms of Use](selo-legal://terms) and the [Selonachipa Privacy Policy](selo-legal://privacy). If they conflict, these terms apply to Riders and Agents.

1.3. Riders and Agents are **independent contractors**. They are not employees, workers, partners or representatives of Selonachipa or Deep Valley Farms Limited, and no employment relationship arises under the Employment Code Act No. 3 of 2019 or any other law.

1.4. Riders and Agents have no authority to act for, speak for, sign for or make any promise on behalf of the Company. Anything they say or do is in their own name only.

1.5. Riders and Agents choose when to go online and whether to accept a job. Selonachipa-branded clothing, bags or ID cards do not make them employees or representatives.

## 2. Eligibility, Documents, Vehicles and Insurance

2.1. Riders and Agents must be at least 18 years old and provide a valid NRC or passport, a recent photo, a working phone number, proof of address and payout account details.

2.2. Riders using a motor vehicle or motorcycle must also hold and keep valid:

- a driver's licence for the vehicle class;

- vehicle registration (white book) and road tax;

- third-party motor insurance as required by the Road Traffic Act No. 11 of 2002; and

- any RTSA permit or certificate of fitness the vehicle needs.

2.3. Riders on bicycles or on foot must still meet the identity requirements in clause 2.1.

2.4. The Company may run identity and background checks and may refuse or remove anyone at its discretion.

2.5. Riders and Agents provide and maintain their own vehicles, fuel, phones, data bundles and equipment at their own cost. They are responsible for insuring themselves, their vehicles and the goods in their care.

## 3. Rider Duties

3.1. Once a Rider accepts a delivery, the Rider is responsible for the goods, food, parcel or document from pickup until handover to the Buyer.

3.2. Riders must:

- collect and deliver promptly, by a safe route, and keep the app's live location on during the job;

- keep goods sealed, clean and undamaged, and never open, swap, tamper with or consume them;

- confirm delivery only when the goods are actually handed over, using the app's proof-of-delivery steps (code, photo or signature);

- obey all road traffic laws, wear a helmet on a motorcycle or bicycle, and never ride under the influence of alcohol or drugs; and

- carry document deliveries confidentially and never read, copy or photograph their contents beyond proof of delivery.

3.3. **No cash payments.** All payments for orders and deliveries are made by mobile money through the Platform. Cash on delivery is not offered. Riders must never ask for, accept or carry cash for an order or delivery. A Rider who takes payment outside the Platform commits a breach of these terms, and any money taken will be treated as obtained by fraud and reported to the Zambia Police Service.

3.4. Riders are liable for goods lost, damaged or stolen while in their care, unless caused by the Seller's packaging or events outside the Rider's control.

## 4. Agent Duties

4.1. Agents may help Users and Sellers register, place orders, make or receive payments, or collect and hand over goods, only within the functions the Platform gives them.

4.2. Agents must:

- give Users accurate information and never promise anything the Platform does not offer;

- verify identity documents genuinely and never register a person using false, borrowed or another person's details;

- record every transaction in the app at the time it happens and issue the receipt or confirmation the app provides;

- never collect cash: every payment must be made by mobile money through the Platform, and any goods received must be held on trust and handed over in full through the approved channel within \[24 hours\]; and

- never charge Users unofficial fees or ask for their PIN, password or one-time password.

4.3. Agents are responsible for any shortage in goods in their care. Any payment an Agent takes outside the Platform is treated as obtained by fraud and will be reported to the Zambia Police Service.

4.4. Where an Agent provides payment or mobile money services, the Agent must also comply with any rules of the licensed payment service provider involved and with the national payment systems laws and Bank of Zambia directives in force.

## 6. Conduct, Fraud, Data, Liability and Indemnity

6.1. Riders and Agents must be honest, polite and professional with Buyers, Sellers and each other. Harassment, threats, violence, discrimination and asking Users for personal favours or money are prohibited.

6.2. **Fraud.** Fake deliveries, false proof of delivery, swapping or stealing goods, taking payment outside the Platform, account sharing, using another person's identity, fake sign-ups, collusion with Sellers or Buyers, and any other dishonest conduct are prohibited. A Rider or Agent who commits fraud or any other offence is **personally and directly liable**. The Company will report it to the Zambia Police Service and support prosecution, including by handing over GPS logs, transaction records, chats and identity documents and giving evidence, as set out in section 6 of the Terms of Use.

6.3. **User data.** Riders and Agents may use Users' names, phone numbers and addresses only to complete the job. They must not save, share or use them afterwards, or contact Users for any other purpose. Misuse breaches the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021, and the Rider or Agent is personally liable.

6.4. **Liability.** To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Company is not liable for any loss, injury, damage, theft or fraud caused by a Rider or Agent, or suffered by a Rider or Agent while doing a job, including road accidents.

6.5. **Indemnity.** Each Rider and Agent indemnifies Deep Valley Farms Limited, its directors, staff and partners against all claims, losses, fines, costs and legal fees arising from their conduct, fraud, accidents, traffic offences, or breach of these terms or the law.
