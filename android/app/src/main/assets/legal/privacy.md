# Selonachipa Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 November 2026_

## 1. Who We Are and What This Policy Covers

1.1. Selonachipa is owned and operated by **Deep Valley Farms Limited** (the **Company**, **we** or **us**), a company incorporated in the Republic of Zambia. The Company is the data controller for personal data processed through the Selonachipa app, website and related services (the **Platform**).

1.2. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with and your rights. It applies to Buyers, Sellers, Riders, Agents and anyone else who uses the Platform.

1.3. We process personal data in line with the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021, the Cyber Security Act No. 3 of 2025, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act No. 4 of 2021 and other applicable Zambian law.

1.4. This Policy forms part of the [Selonachipa Terms of Use](selo-legal://terms). Words defined there have the same meaning here.

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

**User type — Data we collect**

- **All Users:** Name, phone number, email (optional), password/PIN (stored encrypted), device ID, IP address, app usage logs, support messages
- **Buyers:** Delivery addresses, location during an order, order history, payment method details (via payment providers), ratings and reviews
- **Sellers:** Business name, PACRA and TPIN details, owner NRC or passport, business address, product listings and photos, bank or mobile money payout details, sales records
- **Riders:** NRC or passport, driver's licence, vehicle registration and photos, selfie/photo for verification, real-time GPS location while online, delivery records, proof-of-delivery photos and signatures, payout details
- **Agents:** NRC or passport, photo, operating location, transactions and collections handled, payout and float records

2.1. We collect data you give us, data generated when you use the Platform, and data from third parties such as payment providers, mobile network operators and identity verification services.

2.2. We do not collect sensitive personal data (such as health, religion or biometric data beyond a verification photo) unless the law requires it or you give explicit consent.

## 3. How and Why We Use Your Data

**Purpose — Lawful basis**

- **Create, verify and manage your account:** Performance of our contract with you
- **Match Buyers with Sellers, Riders and Agents; process and track orders and deliveries:** Performance of contract
- **Process payments, payouts and refunds:** Performance of contract; legal obligation
- **Verify identity and prevent, detect and investigate fraud and crime:** Legal obligation; legitimate interest in a safe Platform
- **Respond to support requests and resolve disputes:** Performance of contract; legitimate interest
- **Improve the Platform using anonymised statistics:** Legitimate interest
- **Send promotional messages:** Your consent, which you may withdraw at any time
- **Comply with tax, law enforcement, court and regulatory requirements:** Legal obligation

3.1. We do not sell your personal data.

3.2. We may use automated tools to flag suspicious activity. Any decision to close an account for suspected fraud is reviewed by a person, and you may ask us to explain it.

## 4. Who We Share Your Data With

4.1. **Other Users, to complete an order.** Buyers see the Seller's business name and the Rider's first name, photo, vehicle details and live location during delivery. Sellers, Riders and Agents see only the Buyer details needed to complete that order, such as name, phone number and delivery location.

4.2. **Service providers working for us,** including payment service providers, mobile network operators, cloud hosting, SMS and identity-verification suppliers. They may use the data only on our instructions.

4.3. **Law enforcement and authorities.** We share data with the Zambia Police Service, the Zambia Cyber Security Agency, the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, the Zambia Revenue Authority, courts and other authorities where the law requires or allows it. This includes supporting the investigation and prosecution of fraud and other offences committed on the Platform.

4.4. **Business transfers.** If the Company or Selonachipa is sold or restructured, data may pass to the new owner under this Policy.

4.5. Sellers, Riders and Agents who receive Buyer data are responsible for protecting it. They may use it only to complete the order and must not keep, copy, sell or misuse it. Misuse is a breach of the Data Protection Act, for which they are personally liable.

## 5. Retention, Security and Cross-Border Transfers

5.1. **Retention.** We keep account data while your account is active and for 1 (one) year after you delete the app or close your account. Transaction, tax and fraud-related records are kept for up to 10 years or as long as the law, an investigation or a court case requires. After that, data is deleted or anonymised.

5.2. **Security.** We use encryption in transit, access controls, staff confidentiality obligations and monitoring to protect your data, in line with the Cyber Security Act No. 3 of 2025. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify the Data Protection Commissioner and affected Users as the law requires.

5.3. **Your part.** Never share your PIN, password or one-time password. Selonachipa staff will never ask for them.

5.4. **Cross-border transfers.** Some of our technology suppliers may store or process data outside Zambia. We transfer data abroad only as permitted by the Data Protection Act, including where the destination offers adequate protection or appropriate safeguards are in place. \[Confirm the hosting location and any registration with the Data Protection Commissioner.\]

## 6. Your Rights, Children, Changes and Contact

6.1. Under the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021, you have the right to:

- be told how your data is used;

- get a copy of your personal data;

- have inaccurate data corrected;

- have your data deleted, where we no longer need it and the law does not require us to keep it;

- object to or restrict certain processing, including direct marketing; and

- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

6.2. To use these rights, contact us at the details below. We may ask you to prove your identity first. We will respond within the time the law sets.

6.3. If you are unhappy with our response, you may complain to the **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner** of Zambia.

6.4. **Children.** The Platform is not intended for anyone under 18 without a parent's or guardian's consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children without that consent.

6.5. **Changes.** We may update this Policy. The current version is always available in the app and at www.mabala.cloud, and takes effect on publication.
